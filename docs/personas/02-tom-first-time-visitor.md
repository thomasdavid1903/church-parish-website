# Tom, first-time visitor

- **Age:** 29. Pronouns: he/him.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** iPhone (≈ 390×844), found the site through a Google search for "orthodox church chiswick".
- **Tech comfort:** high, but impatient. Makes a judgement within 10 seconds.
- **Background:** not Orthodox. Grew up vaguely Anglican, has been reading about Orthodoxy and wants to attend a service for the first time. Slightly nervous about doing something wrong.

## Goals
1. Understand within seconds what this place is (Orthodox? Russian? Is it OK if I don't speak Russian?).
2. Find when he could come: an evening or Sunday service, and roughly how long it lasts.
3. Find out what to expect: standing, dress code, can he take communion (no), can he just watch.
4. Work out how to get there by public transport, and whether there's parking.
5. Find someone to email if he has questions.

## Behaviour
- Scans headings, skips long paragraphs.
- Taps the first thing that looks like "Visit" or "New here?".
- Will leave if services are listed only in Russian or if jargon ("Vigil", "Hours", "ROCOR") isn't explained anywhere.

## Watch for
- Whether the home page answers "can I come?" without scrolling far.
- Unexplained Orthodox jargon.
- Map/directions, nearest station, bus routes.
- Whether the site feels welcoming to outsiders or only for existing parishioners.
