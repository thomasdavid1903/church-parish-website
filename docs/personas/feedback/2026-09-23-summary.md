## Top issues, ranked

1. **Blocker — visible placeholder on the Donate page.** `[ Online donation form with Gift Aid will appear here. See docs/PROPOSAL.md … ]` shows to the public in both languages. Richard won't give and Andrew would flag it at the council. Hide the "Give online" section until the form exists. (`tools/seed.php`, donate page)
2. **Blocker — no way to message anyone.** Every "Send a message" link on the group pages goes to `/visit/`, which has no email address or form. There's no email address anywhere on the site. Oksana (Sunday School) and Tom hit a dead end.
3. **Major — group pages look unfinished.** "name to be added" / "Details to be added" on Sunday School, Sisterhood, etc. The Sunday School page has no ages, times or sign-up. Either fill them in before the preview or hide the Contact block until it has content.
4. **Major — phone numbers aren't tappable.** Clergy numbers on Home, Clergy and Visit are plain text, not `tel:` links. On a phone, Galina can't tap to call.
5. **Major — directions are vague.** `/visit/` has an embedded Google map, but the text only says stations and buses are "within walking distance". Name them (Chiswick Park, Gunnersbury, Turnham Green; bus routes) and mention parking. *Partly fixed 23 Sep: "Open in Google Maps" links added to the home address card, the Visit address card and the footer (EN + RU).*
6. **Major — Russian pages show English.**
   - News dates read "15 JULY 2026".
   - The browser title on the Russian home page is entirely English, and other Russian pages end in "– London Orthodox Cathedral".
   - Likely causes: the WordPress `ru_RU` language pack isn't installed in Playground (install it on Hostinger too), and the site title isn't translated through Polylang *Strings translations*.
7. **Major — Services page depends only on the calendar.** The regular service times (Vigil 5 pm, Liturgy 10 am…) appear on Home but not on the Services page. If the embed fails, or on a small phone where a MONTH view is cramped, there's nothing to read. Add the regular-times card to `/services/`, use `AGENDA` mode on narrow screens, and add an "Open in Google Calendar" link.
8. **Minor — Sunday School missing from the home page "Parish life" cards**, even though the latest news post is about it. Oksana's most likely target.
9. **Minor — no H1 on the home page.** Headings start at H2 "Welcome". Screen-reader users (Sam) and SEO expect the Cathedral name as H1 on the front page.
10. **Minor — inconsistencies Andrew would spot:**
    - "four to five times a week" (Home) vs "four Liturgies each week" (Services).
    - "Annual Parish Assembly" vs "Annual Parish Meeting" in two news posts.
    - Three different payee names: *Russian Orthodox Church Abroad (London)* (bank), *London Russian Orthodox Church* (cheques), and the charity name in the footer.
    - Phone formats vary (`+44 771 432 4482`, `+44 7876 474358`).
    - Hieromonk Theodore and Deacon Sergei have no photo while the others do.
11. **Minor — jargon not explained for newcomers:** Vigil, Akathist, solea, iconostasis, Holy Mysteries. Also missing: how long a service lasts, and whether visitors can just stand at the back and watch.
12. **Minor — donation details:** there's no payment reference for general donations (Sunday School has one), no account name for the Sunday School account, and the charity number appears only in the footer, not beside the bank details.

## What worked well

- Language switch keeps you on the equivalent page (checked Home, Visit, Donate, Services, Sunday School, News).
- The home page is clear and fast: service times, address, shop hours and main contacts are all near the top in both languages.
- Russian text reads naturally overall. The menu button says "Меню" in Russian.
- Skip link, header/nav/main/footer landmarks, `lang="ru-RU"` on Russian pages, and alt text on clergy photos are all in place. The calendar iframe has a title.
- Group leader restrictions are thorough: edit only assigned pages and their translations, no delete, no new pages, a trimmed menu and a "Your group pages" dashboard widget.

Per-persona detail is in the other files in this folder.
