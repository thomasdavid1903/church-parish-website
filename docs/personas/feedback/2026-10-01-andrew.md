# Fr Deacon Andrew — 2026-10-01 (photo update)

**Verdict:** With difficulty. The photos are a big improvement in dignity. A few placements need sorting before the council sees it.

## Goals
| Goal | Result | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dignified and Orthodox at first glance | Yes | The new home banner is the best image on the site. It's clearly our Cathedral. |
| Credit is correct and respectful | Partly | The English is correct. Russian should be «протодиакон Сергий Баранов», not the Latin-script name. |

## What worked
- Every new photo is of our Cathedral. None of the other churches from the album slipped in.
- The credit is understated. It reads like a signature, not a watermark.

## Problems
1. **Major** On the Clergy banner on a phone, the credit covers faces. (`style.css`: `.parish-photo-credit`. On a 375 px banner it's about 275 px wide.)
2. **Minor** The Russian credit is in Latin letters.
3. **Minor** The Sacraments banner choice: raise with the Rector.
4. **Minor** In the enlarged gallery view (lightbox) there's no credit, so anyone screenshotting the large photo loses the attribution.

## In their words
> "Fr Sergei's photos are superb. Just don't write his name across the Bishop's clergy."

## Suggested fixes
- On narrow screens, make the credit smaller and shorter (e.g. just the photographer's name). Or place it under the banner instead of over it.
- Russian credit name.
- Show the credit in the lightbox too.
