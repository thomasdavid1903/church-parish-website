## Problems
1. **[Not an issue, per site owner]** Only approved people (the clergy) may photograph during services, so the service photos are consistent with the rule. *Original note:* **The gallery text contradicts the photos.** The gallery says *"Photography is not permitted during services, so these photos are taken before or after them"*. But it shows "The faithful holding candles at a service", "A hierarchical service in the Cathedral" and a holy-water blessing. A parishioner will point this out. Either reword it (e.g. "taken by the parish photographer with the blessing of the clergy") or drop those photos (`tools/seed.php:923`).
2. **[Minor] The Choir page banner is "The faithful holding candles at a service".** It shows no choir. There's a choir-music photo, `choir-music.jpg`, that fits better (`tools/seed.php:1376`).
3. **[Minor] Children's photos** (Sunday School with the Bishop, Youth Group). Ask whether the parish has consent on file.
4. **[Minor] Daily Readings (English) shows the feed's typos**, e.g. "Hieromartyr Nicholas diacon". Individual words such as "Skepsis" (a town) are links. It reads a little like a machine feed. It's acceptable, but worth knowing it's a third-party source.
5. **[Still open]** "Annual Parish Assembly" vs "Annual Parish Meeting" in the news.
