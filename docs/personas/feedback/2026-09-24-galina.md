# Galina, elderly parishioner — 24 September 2026 (round 4)

Tested at 360×740, Russian, using the **Русский** switch. The new photo hero, page banners and the new **Чтения дня** (Daily Readings) page were the focus.

**Verdict:** With difficulty. She finds Sunday's times, but only after scrolling past a large photo. She can't tell from the site that Sunday is a Great Feast.

## What worked
- The switch reads «Русский», is 44px tall and sits at the top. It takes her to `/ru/glavnaya/`.
- The hero is in good Russian: «Русская Православная Церковь Заграницей», «Добро пожаловать в Лондонский православный собор». The photo of the frescoes looks like "our church".
- **Чтения дня** is in the menu under Богослужения. Its date line is correct and natural: «Четверг, 24 сентября 2026 г. · 11 сентября по старому стилю · Седмица 17-я по Пятидесятнице. Глас 7».
- The photos have Russian alt text in the home-page strip and in the gallery.
- No horizontal scrolling at 360px.

## Problems
1. **[Major] The feast isn't named on the Russian readings page.** `parish_readings_ru_line()` deliberately drops feast names, and the saints section is English-only, so it's hidden in Russian. On a Great Feast she sees a fast day and some Bible references, nothing more. The weekday reading notes are also dropped: today's Gal./Lk. readings are for St Silouan in English but have no label in Russian. As a result she can't tell which readings belong to the day and which to the saint.
2. **[Major, regression] Service times are pushed down by the hero.** The hero is 457px tall on a phone. There's also a 48px empty gap between the header (ends at 141px) and the hero (starts at 189px). The "Regular services" card that round 3 moved up is now just below the first screen again.
3. **[Minor] Page banners push content down on every page.** On Богослужения the banner is 185px, so "Регулярные богослужения" starts at 789px, below the fold.
4. **[Minor] Slow data:** the home page loads about 950KB of photos (9 images at 768px). The hero and the first two strip photos load straight away.
5. **[Minor] The readings references aren't links in Russian.** Only the line to «Азбука веры» is. She would expect to tap «Мф. 11:27–30» and read it.

## In their words
> «Красиво, наш собор. Но время службы опять внизу. А в воскресенье Воздвижение — почему тут не написано?»

## Suggested fixes
- Russian readings: map the twelve Great Feasts, Pascha and the major commemorations to their Russian names, e.g. "Universal Exaltation" → «Воздвижение Креста Господня». Show the name in the date box, and translate the per-reading notes. See `wp-content/plugins/parish-core/includes/readings.php:171` and `:190`.
- Hero on phones: lower the height (`style.css:337`, `min-height: 380px`). Or place a compact "Liturgy Sunday 10:00 · Vigil Saturday 17:00" line inside the hero, or put the services card above it on narrow screens.
- Remove the gap above the hero (`templates/front-page.html:3`, `main` top padding).
- Consider linking Russian references to azbyka.ru for each reading.
