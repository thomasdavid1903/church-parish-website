## What the personas want improved, by priority

### 1. The calendar is unreadable on a phone — Galina, Oksana, Tom
At 375px the MONTH view cuts every service to a stub: "18:15 Ve", "Prophe", "Martyr". Galina can't tell a Vigil from an Akathist.
- Use `AGENDA` (list) view on narrow screens and keep MONTH on desktop. For example, render both iframes and switch with a CSS media query, or add a `mobileView` attribute to the block. (`parish-core/includes/calendar.php`, `blocks/service-calendar/block.json`)
- Put the "Regular services" times on the Services page itself, not only on Home. Then there's always something readable if the embed is slow or blocked.
- Add an "Open in Google Calendar" link under the embed, so people can subscribe on their phone.

### 2. The header takes half the phone screen — Galina, Tom, Oksana
- The header is 327px tall at 375px wide and 349px at 320px: about half the first screen. On the Russian pages it's 377px, because the subtitle wraps to three lines.
- On Services, Galina has to scroll past the logo, the subtitle and a separate full-width "Menu" row before she sees anything.
- Suggestions for phones:
  - Put the cross, the short name and the Menu button on one row.
  - Hide or shorten the italic subtitle ("of the Nativity…").
  - Reduce the header padding.
- The "Menu" label also sits below and to the right of the ☰ icon instead of beside it. (`parts/header.html`, `style.css`)

### 3. Phone numbers should be tappable — Galina, Oksana, Tom
Clergy numbers on Home, Clergy and Visit are plain text. Make them `tel:` links, and use one format throughout (`+44 7xxx xxx xxx`; currently `+44 771 432 4482` and `+44 7876 474358` differ). (`tools/seed.php`)

### 4. Russian pages still show English — Galina, Oksana
- News dates show "15 JULY 2026" on the Russian home and News pages. Install the WordPress `ru_RU` language pack, both in `blueprint.json` for dev and on Hostinger.
- Browser tab titles end in "– London Orthodox Cathedral", and the Russian home page's title is entirely English. Translate the site title and tagline under Polylang → Strings translations. The seed could do this.
- Search results on `/ru/` say "Search results for:" in English.

### 5. Small tap targets — Galina, Sam
- The **English / Русский** buttons are 26px tall, and the card links ("Full schedule", "All clergy", "Open in Google Maps") are 22px. Footer links are 18px.
- Aim for at least 44px tall. Padding on the language switcher and on the card links would do it.

### 6. Clergy page is a very long scroll on a phone — Andrew, Oksana
- Each priest is a full-width card with a portrait about 380px tall. Six clergy make the page about 2,950px long, so Oksana scrolls a long way to find the priest she needs.
- On phones, use a small photo (about 96px) beside the name, role and phone number. Keep the large cards on desktop.

### 7. Keyboard focus isn't styled — Sam
- The theme has no `:focus-visible` rules, so it relies on the browser default. That default is hard to see on the burgundy top bar and the cream background.
- Add a clear focus ring, for example `outline: 3px solid var(--wp--preset--color--accent); outline-offset: 2px`, to links and buttons.
- The menu button has `aria-expanded` but no `aria-controls`.

### 8. No H1 on the home page — Sam, Andrew
The first heading is H2 "Welcome". Make the Cathedral name the H1 on the front page, or add a visually hidden one. The home-page H3 cards (Regular services, Address…) also sit under the "Latest news" H2, so to a screen reader they sound like news items.

### 9. News articles are a dead end — Marina, Oksana
- A single post has no "← All news" link. The only link is to the neighbouring post, and it doesn't say "Previous" or "Next", so it reads like a related article. Add a "Back to news" link and label the previous/next links. (`templates/single.html`)
- Posts have no featured image when shared on WhatsApp or Facebook, and there's no meta description. Add Open Graph tags (e.g. the free Yoast/Rank Math, or a small `wp_head` hook) with the Cathedral's photo as the default image. Parishioners mostly share news on WhatsApp.
- There's no site icon (favicon). Use the Orthodox cross.

### 10. Search is hidden and noisy — Tom, Richard
- There's no search box anywhere except the 404 page.
- Results include *pages* with a publish date ("23 September 2026 · Choir…"), which looks odd. The search template shows post dates for everything.
- Either add a small search to the footer and hide dates for pages, or drop search entirely. For a site this size, the menu is enough.

### 11. Content gaps in finished pages — Tom, Oksana
- **Visit → Getting here** still doesn't name the stations, bus routes or parking. The new Google Maps link helps, but Tom wants "10 minutes' walk from Gunnersbury" in words.
- **Sunday School** isn't among the home page's Parish life cards, although the latest news post is about it.
- **Jargon**: a one-line explanation each for Vigil, Akathist and Hours would help first-time visitors, and so would how long services last.
- "four to five times a week" (Home) vs "four Liturgies each week" (Services): pick one.

## What's working well now
- The Google Maps link sits beside the address on Home, Visit and in the footer, in both languages.
- No horizontal scrolling at 320px on any page tested.
- The desktop layout is calm and readable: two columns with the service times card at the top right.
- The 404 page is bilingual and helpful.
- `hreflang` links for English and Russian are in place, and the language switch keeps you on the equivalent page.
- Text contrast passes WCAG AA across the home page.
