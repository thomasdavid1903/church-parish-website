## What worked
- The visitor guidance is clear and honest: dress, men on the right and women on the left, communion only for prepared Orthodox Christians, and visitors can take the blessed bread.

## Problems
1. **Major** "Getting here" is too vague. It should name the stations, bus routes and parking.
2. **Major** No email or contact form. A nervous first-timer won't cold-call a priest's mobile.
3. **Minor** Jargon: Vigil, Akathist, solea, iconostasis, Holy Mysteries are all unexplained.
4. **Minor** Nothing about service length (a Vigil is about 2–3 hours) or whether he can arrive late or stand at the back.
5. **Minor** The "New to Orthodoxy?" link is inside the second paragraph. A "New here?" button near the top would catch scanners.

## In his words
> "Nice and clear about what to wear, but I still don't know which station to get off at."

## Suggested fixes
- Expand "Getting here" on the Visit page. Add a short "Your first visit" section covering service length, a glossary and a general email.
