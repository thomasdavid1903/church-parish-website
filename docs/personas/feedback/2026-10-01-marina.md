## What worked
- The help text under the field is clear and gives an example.
- Leaving the field empty means no credit, which is easy to understand.

## Problems
1. **Major** Each photo is in the library twice, so she'll fix one copy and the other language still shows the old credit. (`tools/seed.php` `sd_photo_id()`: the `-ru` copy is imported separately.)
2. **Minor** The field is easy to miss and too narrow. (`photo-credit.php`: `attachment_fields_to_edit`.)
3. **Minor** It's not clear whether to type the Russian or the English name. One field covers both languages.

## In their words
> «Я исправила подпись, а на русской странице всё по-старому. Почему?»

## Suggested fixes
- Add an EN/RU pair of credit fields, or copy the credit to the photo's other-language copy on save.
- Put the credit field right after Alternative Text, at full width.
- Add one line about photo credits to `docs/EDITING-GUIDE.md`.
