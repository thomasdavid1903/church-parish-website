# Sam — 2026-10-01 (photo update)

**Verdict:** Yes, with noise. The photos are well described. But the credits are read out as loose text, 16 times in a row in the gallery.

## Goals
| Goal | Result | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful alt text on new photos | Yes | Every new photo has a specific alt in both languages ("The Bishop giving Holy Communion"). The home cover image has `alt=""` because it's decorative, which is correct. |
| Gallery browsable by screen reader | With difficulty | After every image, NVDA reads "Photo: Protodeacon Sergei Baranoff" as a separate line of text: 16 identical lines. It isn't tied to the image as a caption. |
| Russian voice for Russian credit | Partly | The credit sits inside `lang="ru-RU"`. But the name is English, so the Russian voice mispronounces it. |
| Credit contrast | Mostly | White italic text with a shadow over a dark fade. It's readable on dark photos. On bright areas (e.g. the Visit banner's white wall) contrast likely drops below AA for 11–13 px text. |

## What worked
- Alt text is specific and translated.

## Problems
1. **Minor** The credit is a plain `<span>` inside `<figure>`, not a `<figcaption>` or `aria-describedby`. That means it's announced as separate text, repeated over and over. (`photo-credit.php`: `parish_photo_credit_html()`.)
2. **Minor** Contrast on bright photos isn't guaranteed. (`style.css`: `.parish-photo-credit` background fades to 0.)

## In their words
> "I know who took the photos after the first one. I don't need to hear it sixteen times."

## Suggested fixes
- In galleries, hide the repeated credit from screen readers (`aria-hidden="true"`) and add one line under the gallery: "Photos: Protodeacon Sergei Baranoff". Or use `<figcaption>`, so it belongs to its image.
- Keep the fade darker right behind the text.
