## Problems
1. **Blocker (new)** On a 375px phone the page is 443px wide. The search box that moved into the top bar no longer shrinks, so the bar pushes «Русский» off the right edge and the page slides sideways when she taps. She doesn't scroll sideways, so she never finds Russian. Same at 320 and 414px. The last commit (3f3e585) fits at 375px. Cause: `.parish-topbar .parish-search input[type="search"] { width: auto; }` in the mobile media query of `wp-content/themes/parish-cathedral/style.css` (~line 182): the input keeps its default ~20-character width.
2. **Major (new)** On «Контакты», the phone numbers (what she wants) come after the long form on a phone. She'd assume there is no number.
3. **Minor** «Вечерня, вс–чт» is an abbreviation she may not read. «воскресенье – четверг» is clearer.
4. **Minor** «Пока не получено» four times on the home page reads as unfinished and odd to a parishioner.
