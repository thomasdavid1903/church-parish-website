## What worked
- **Gallery lightbox:** each photo has an "Enlarge 1 of 15" button with a visible focus ring. Enter opens a `role="dialog"` `aria-modal` labelled "Enlarged image 1 of 15: <alt>". Escape closes it and focus goes back to the button. In Russian the labels read «Увеличение 1 из 15».
- All content photos have meaningful alt text. The hero image is correctly decorative (`alt=""`).
- Daily Readings: H1 → H2 sections, with the troparia in a native `<details>`.

## Problems
1. **[Major] English alt text on every Russian page banner.** The banners on Богослужения, Таинства, Приходская жизнь, all five group pages, Духовенство, Контакты and Пожертвования read out in English with a Russian voice, e.g. "Sunday School children with the Bishop". The seed sets one shared attachment (with English alt) as the featured image for both languages (`tools/seed.php:1369–1389`). The inline photos use `sd_photo()`, which writes the right language.
2. **[Minor, regression] Two H1s on the home page.** The header's site title is H1 on the front page ("The front page has no other H1", `inc/header-footer.php:78`), and the new hero adds "Welcome to the London Orthodox Cathedral" as a second H1. One of them should become a `p`.
3. **[Minor] Headings skip from H1 to H3** on Visit Us / Контакты, Parish Life, all five group pages, Photo gallery and Donate. (The clergy page was fixed in round 3.)
4. **[Minor] Hero text contrast depends on the photo.** The gold eyebrow (#e9d9b0, 14px, uppercase) and white text sit on a 60% dark overlay. Over the brightest part of the fresco the eyebrow can fall to about 3:1. Raise the dim to 70%, or add a gradient behind the text (`style.css:321`).
5. **[Minor] The links list on Daily Readings (English)** includes single-word saint links such as "Sergius", "Herman", "Skepsis" and "Demetrian". Out of context they're unclear.

## In their words
> "The gallery's done properly. But the Russian pages describe their photos in English, and the home page has two main headings again."

## Suggested fixes
- Give each language its own attachment (or set the Russian alt per page). Simplest: import the photo twice in `sd_photo_id()` with the Russian alt for `ru` and set that as the Russian page's thumbnail.
- On the front page, make the header title a `p` now that the hero has the H1 (`inc/header-footer.php:78`).
- Change the H3 section headings on those pages to H2 in `tools/seed.php`.
