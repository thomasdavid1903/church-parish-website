## What worked
- The hero has two clear buttons: **Service times** and **Plan your visit**. Tom taps the second one.
- The Daily Readings page is a nice extra. The English version names the saints and feasts, the readings link to their texts, and the troparia are tucked into a "Troparia and kontakia" disclosure.
- The Photo gallery makes the parish feel alive and welcoming.

## Problems
1. **[Major, content, still open] No email address.** He'd rather email than phone a priest's mobile.
2. **[Minor, regression] "New to Orthodoxy?" link moved from 608px to 1,356px.** The hero's "Plan your visit" button covers most of this, but the reassuring sentence is now two screens down.
3. **[Minor] Daily Readings doesn't say which reading is the Epistle and which the Gospel.** On 27 Sept every reading is labelled just "The Universal Exaltation". The feast name is also only in the "Saints and feasts" section further down, not in the date box.
4. **[Minor] The gallery says "Click a photo"** on a phone ("Tap" or "Select" would fit). Its last photo sits alone and is full width at 390px (15 photos in 2 columns).
5. **[Still open, content]** Nearest stations, bus numbers, parking, and roughly how long the Vigil and Liturgy last.
