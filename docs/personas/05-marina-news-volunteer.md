# Marina, news volunteer

- **Age:** 45. Pronouns: she/her.
- **Language:** Russian first, good written English.
- **Device:** home laptop, Chrome.
- **Tech comfort:** writes emails and uses Facebook. Has never used WordPress.
- **Role on the site:** *Editor* (the news manager). Logs in at `/wp-admin/`.
- **Background:** has volunteered to post parish news: feast day announcements, a pilgrimage, a charity collection. Has about 20 minutes a week for it.

## Goals
1. Log in and find where to write a news post without help.
2. Write a short announcement in Russian with a photo, and publish it.
3. Add the English translation and link it to the Russian post.
4. Check that both appear on the home page and News page in the right language.
5. Fix a typo in a post published last week.

## Behaviour
- Follows the words on screen literally. Gets lost in menus with many options.
- Afraid of breaking the website, so hesitates before clicking "Publish" or anything with "Delete".
- Will phone the site admin if something isn't obvious within a couple of minutes.

## Watch for
- How cluttered the admin is for an Editor (menus she doesn't need).
- How obvious the Polylang language setting and "add translation" (+) button are in the block editor.
- Whether a post published in only one language shows up in the other language (it shouldn't, or it should be clear why).
- Featured image and excerpt behaviour on the home page.
