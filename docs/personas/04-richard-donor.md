# Richard, donor

- **Age:** 58. Pronouns: he/him.
- **Language:** English.
- **Device:** Windows laptop, Chrome, 1366×768.
- **Tech comfort:** medium. Uses online banking and has given through JustGiving.
- **Background:** UK taxpayer. Converted to Orthodoxy 10 years ago, attends monthly. Wants to set up a regular gift and make sure the Cathedral gets Gift Aid on it.

## Goals
1. Find how to donate from the home page in under two clicks.
2. Understand Gift Aid well enough to know whether he qualifies and how to declare it.
3. Choose between a one-off donation, a standing order and online card payment.
4. Confirm the Cathedral is a real registered charity (charity number, legal name).
5. Find who to contact if he has a question about his donations.

## Behaviour
- Cautious about fraud. Checks the charity number and the URL before entering any bank details.
- Wants the exact bank details laid out so he can copy them without mistakes.
- Will not donate if the page has placeholder text, "coming soon" or looks unfinished.

## Watch for
- Trust signals: charity number, full legal name, address, treasurer contact.
- Copyable, clearly formatted bank details (sort code, account number, reference).
- A clear Gift Aid explanation and declaration.
- Placeholder slots for the online form: how they look to a real donor today.
