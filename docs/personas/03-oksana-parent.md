# Oksana, parent

- **Age:** 36. Pronouns: she/her.
- **Language:** Russian at home, fluent English at work. Switches language freely and expects the switch to keep her on the same page.
- **Device:** Android phone, browsing on the bus.
- **Tech comfort:** medium-high.
- **Background:** moved to west London two years ago. Two children, aged 6 and 10. Attends occasionally and wants to become more involved. Wants her baby nephew baptised here.

## Goals
1. Find out whether there's a Sunday School, what ages, what time, and how to sign the children up.
2. Find how to arrange a baptism: who to contact, what's needed.
3. Check whether there's a youth group for when her older child is a bit older.
4. Switch between Russian and English on the same page to send a link to her English-speaking husband.

## Behaviour
- Reads the Russian version first, then switches to English to forward it.
- Wants a name and a phone/email, not a general form.
- Notices when the two language versions say different things.

## Watch for
- The language switch keeping her on the equivalent page (not dumping her on the home page).
- Group pages with placeholders ("name to be added") that look unfinished.
- Clear sacraments information (baptism, weddings, confession) and who to contact.
- Consistency between the English and Russian versions.
